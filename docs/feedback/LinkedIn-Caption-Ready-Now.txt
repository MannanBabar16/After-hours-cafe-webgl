Been working on a little café prototype called After Hours Café ☕

I made this to test ideas for a PC game I want to build and eventually bring to Steam. It's an early browser prototype, but I wanted to see how making coffee and actually running the place would feel together.

Right now you can:
• make and serve six coffee recipes
• bake cookies and handle indoor + drive-through orders
• buy supplies, restock equipment and keep track of money
• upgrade equipment and customize the café
• use Bomi for table deliveries, with Mochi cleaning up spills
• switch between the diorama view and first person
• meet regulars, hang out with the café cat and carry your progress into the next evening

There's rain, music and sound effects too. Headphones help with the vibe.

I'd love some honest feedback before I take this further. What feels good, what gets annoying, and what would make you want to come back to a game like this?

You can try it here: https://after-hours-cafe-webgl.vercel.app
