READ THE NUMBERS FAIRLY

Good data, honest context.

Waiting for your first players.

  • A visitor means an anonymous browser profile, not a verified person. Reloads count as visits; multiple sessions in this range count as returning.
  • Play time excludes hidden tabs, pauses, help, settings and title/summary screens. It is measured foreground time, not proof of attention.
  • Self-reported gameplay telemetry can be blocked or lost on exit. Bots are filtered on a best-effort basis.
  • Game money is simulated café revenue. It is not real income.
