Your visit,

This prototype uses first-party analytics to understand what works: visits, play time, coffee made, orders served and which features people try. It helps shape the future PC game.

We store a random browser identifier, hashed on the server, plus a session identifier, event timestamps, coarse device/browser categories and the referring website hostname. We do not collect your café name, saves, email, keystrokes, precise location or IP address in the analytics records. Hosting services process network requests separately.

Your save stays in your browser. Analytics data is stored privately and viewed by the developer. It is self-reported, not a verified count of people. Data starts with the October 20, 2026 update.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable collection. You can also turn it off here for this browser. Reload any open game tabs after changing this setting. Blocking analytics never prevents play.
